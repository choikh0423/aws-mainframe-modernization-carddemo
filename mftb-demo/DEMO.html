CardDemo modernization, driven by evidence

AWS CardDemo (app/, 240 files) analysed with the mftb mainframe toolbox @ 419573e: deterministic scanners produce the inventory, Devin adds cited judgment, and the modernization plan is built only from that store.

The problem

A modernization plan is only as good as the inventory under it. Guessed call targets, unknown job order and assumed environment facts are where migrations slip.

  • CardDemo mixes CICS screens, nightly and monthly batch, VSAM, DB2, IMS and MQ across one core app and three optional extensions.
  • Questions a customer asks first: what is in scope, what depends on what, what runs at night, which screens write which data, and what we do not know yet.
  • Rule of this demo: every fact cites path:line or a store file; anything unproven stays an open finding or evidence request.

Top evidence requests for the customer

  1. Environments, test LPAR and data masking policy (the 4 high profile findings).
  2. Confirm the 9 ASSUMED profile fields (z/OS level, RACF, Endevor, DFSORT, compiler, ...).
  3. Who delivers DALYTRAN.PS, and when.
  4. Missing source: HLASM COBDATFT/MVSWAIT, REPROCT control cards, TXT2PDF REXX, BMS, IMS DBDs.
  5. Keep the CICS file-close batch window, or allow concurrent online/batch?
  6. Implement fees in INTCALC, or migrate as-is?
